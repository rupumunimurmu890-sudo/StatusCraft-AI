Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

StatusCraft AI ("we", "our", "the app") is a free tool that lets you generate AI-written quotes and status images. This page explains what information we collect and how it is used.

1. Photos you upload

If you choose to upload a photo, it is processed entirely in your own browser to create your status image. Your photo is not uploaded to, or stored on, our servers.

2. AI-generated quotes

Quote text is generated using Cloudflare Workers AI based on the language, category, and mood you select. Generated quotes are stored anonymously (text only, no personal information) to avoid repeating the same quote to different users. This storage does not include your name, photo, or device information.

3. Name and other optional inputs

If you type your name into the app, it is used only to render it on your generated status image in your browser. It is not sent to or stored on our servers.

4. Local storage on your device

The app stores a small list of your recently generated quotes on your own device (browser local storage) so it can avoid repeating them to you. This data stays on your device and is not accessible to us or to other users.

5. Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics or Google Analytics) to understand overall usage patterns, such as how many people visit the app or which features are used most. This data is aggregated and not used to identify individual users.

6. Advertising

If ads are shown in the app in the future, they may be served through third-party ad networks (such as Google AdSense), which may use cookies or similar technologies as described in their own privacy policies.

7. Third-party services we use

  • Cloudflare Workers AI — for generating quotes and background images
  • Cloudflare Workers KV — for anonymous quote-repeat prevention

8. Children's privacy

StatusCraft AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
